Well, I just posted about the [Cube Calculator](/cubecalculator) and now I am adding a second gadget that I have been wanting as well. I like to keep track of the decks that have been drafted and while there are plenty of tools already out there, none of them are quite what I want. An honorable mention here is [Snap Drafter](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dbrown.mtg_draft_tracker), which allows you to take a picture and automatically scan the cards you've drafted. However, it only really works with English cards and even then tends to miss 1-2 cards depending on lighting etc. I used it for a bit but it was a hassle to get it right every time.

In the [Cubelogger](/cubelogger), I stuck to manual input. However, I aimed to make it as easy as possible. You link to your [CubeCobra](https://cubecobra.com) ID 
- Enter a CubeCobra cube ID or URL.
- Choose how many decks were drafted.
- Select the cards via a clickable interface that ended up in their deck.
- Save and export the session.

The tool stores the results locally in your browser and can export the draft as structured JSON or a simple text list.

**Note:** This is another tool that is entirely vibecoded to not spend too much time on it. Let me know what isn't working so I can go back and fix it manually.
